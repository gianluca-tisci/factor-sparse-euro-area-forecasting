Quarterly information has selective predictive value. Manufacturing improves in the DFM family; inflation does not improve systematically. LASSO improves descriptively for energy and core, without nominal 5% evidence. Manufacturing DFM and FarmPredict-DFM improve by similar amounts. This is consistent with a factor channel, but does not identify it: M to MQ changes both the factor panel and the sparse dictionary. A fixed-factor quarterly-dictionary ablation would be needed to separate those channels.

The best monthly-only manufacturing method remains FarmPredict (PCA+VAR+LASSO). The MQ improvement of DFM does not eliminate that performance gap. The PCA variant is not estimated in MQ, so these results do not identify the effect of adding quarterly information to PCA-based FarmPredict.

The cumulative FarmPredict/LASSO ranking reverses mainly during 2022-2023. In the listed inversion episodes, the DFM-based forecast remains positive while the realized monthly energy-price change is negative. Direct LASSO forecasts are less positive or negative. Large squared errors dominate earlier smaller gains. Results in 2024-2025 are nearly tied, so the high final cumulative ratio does not mean LASSO continuously improves at the end of the sample. FarmPredict and DFM forecasts remain very close: the sparse residual correction does not materially repair these factor forecast errors.

These observations are descriptive and do not establish which selected predictors cause the difference. That question requires a separate selection/ablation analysis. No causal claim about an economic shock or a general rejection of quarterly information follows from these diagnostics.
